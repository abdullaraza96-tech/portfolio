01DOMAIN

Ride-hailing operations

Bookings, revenue, cancellations, vehicle mix, payment behavior, location activity and captain performance.

02PROBLEM

What is driving performance?

Bring fragmented booking metrics into a decision-friendly view and surface where operational performance needs attention.

03KPI LOGIC

Measure what matters

Total bookings, revenue, average fare, completed rides, cancellation rate, payment share, vehicle revenue and time trends.

WHAT THE SAMPLE SHOWS

Dashboard observations

01

Cab contributes the highest vehicle revenue at roughly ₹18K, followed by Auto and Bike.

02

UPI has the largest payment share at about 36%; Cash and Card are each around 32%.

03

A 42% cancellation rate is the clearest operational issue visible in the overview and deserves deeper root-cause analysis.

BUSINESS THINKING / NEXT

Questions I’d investigate next

  • Which time slots, locations or vehicle types drive the most cancellations?
  • Do peak booking periods have enough captain availability?
  • Which pickup zones combine high demand with weak completion rates?
  • Does payment mode correlate with cancellation or fare patterns?

02 / ANALYTICAL METHOD

Not charts first. Questions first.

My project workflow is organized so I can explain the dataset, the problem, every step I took and the final outcome clearly in an interview.

01

Define the business question

Choose the domain, understand what the operation is trying to measure and identify the KPIs before designing visuals.

DOMAIN · PROBLEM · KPI
02

Audit & clean the data

Inspect shape, columns and types; check missing values and duplicates; save a clean analysis-ready dataset.

df.info() · isnull() · drop_duplicates()
03

Analyze patterns

Use Pandas and NumPy for revenue, booking status, vehicles, payments, captains, locations and daily patterns.

groupby() · value_counts() · agg()
04

Build the decision layer

Turn the analysis into Power BI KPIs, filters and detail views that help a viewer understand what matters quickly.
